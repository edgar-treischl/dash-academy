# Understanding Data

Data comes in many forms and serves different purposes. Let's explore the main types.

## Quantitative vs. Qualitative

### Quantitative Data
Numerical data that can be measured and analyzed mathematically.

**Examples:**
- Sales revenue: $1,250,000
- Customer count: 500
- Page load time: 2.3 seconds

### Qualitative Data
Descriptive information about qualities or characteristics.

**Examples:**
- Customer feedback: "Great user experience, but slow checkout"
- Product descriptions: "Lightweight, durable, water-resistant"
- Interview responses: "Employees value flexibility and autonomy"

## Structured vs. Unstructured

**Structured Data** follows a clear format (like spreadsheets or databases)
**Unstructured Data** doesn't follow a fixed format (like emails, videos, images)

## Primary vs. Secondary

**Primary Data**: Information you collect directly from the source
**Secondary Data**: Information collected by others that you use for analysis

> 💡 **Tip:** Most real-world analysis combines multiple data types. Understanding what you have helps you choose the right analysis method.
