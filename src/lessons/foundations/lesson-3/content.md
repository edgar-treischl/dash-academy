# Data in Organizations

Every organization generates massive amounts of data daily. Understanding how it flows and who uses it is essential.

## Data Sources in Business

### Customer Data
- Purchase history
- Browsing behavior
- Support tickets
- Feedback and reviews

### Operational Data
- Sales transactions
- Inventory levels
- Employee performance
- System logs

### Market Data
- Industry trends
- Competitor analysis
- Economic indicators
- Customer demographics

## Who Uses Data?

| Role | Primary Focus | Example Use |
|------|---------------|-------------|
| Sales | Revenue growth | Identify high-value customers |
| Marketing | Customer acquisition | Optimize campaign spend |
| Operations | Efficiency | Reduce production downtime |
| Leadership | Strategic planning | Market expansion decisions |
| Finance | Budgeting | Forecast cash flow |

## Data Governance

Organizations need systems to manage data:
- **Data Ownership**: Who's responsible for each dataset?
- **Data Quality**: Is it accurate and complete?
- **Data Security**: Who can access what?
- **Data Privacy**: Compliance with regulations
